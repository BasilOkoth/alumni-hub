# Household privacy & editing update

This build changes household cover from large public cards to a compact, private, expandable record under the principal member.

## What changed
- Public member pages no longer reveal household names, contribution balances, payout numbers or support history.
- Authorized admin/committee views get a compact household summary with **View household** disclosure.
- Children are grouped under one collapsible section so multiple children do not consume the page.
- Household members can be edited by an administrator.
- Any protected household edit resets verification to **Pending**, requiring re-verification.
- `Remove` was replaced by **Deactivate cover**. Historical records are retained.
- Principal member details can be edited from a collapsed admin section.
- Payout details and support history are also collapsed/private.

This is intentionally a least-privilege web view. Members can continue to access their own household information through the private WhatsApp assistant until a dedicated member-login area is added.
