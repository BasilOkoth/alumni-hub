WHAT CAN BE SUPPORTED?

Define support before a crisis.

The committee sets the benefit, waiting period and annual claim limit for each relationship and event type. Routine expenses do not automatically become welfare claims.

01

Bereavement

Principal member or a verified spouse, child, mother or father.

02

Serious illness

Defined major illness requiring documented treatment, not ordinary outpatient visits.

03

Hospitalisation

Support can be tied to admission criteria agreed by the group.

04

Accident / emergency

Exceptional urgent support with evidence and a defined financial cap.
